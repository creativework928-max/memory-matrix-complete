# Game Rules

## INITIATE

- Grid: 4 × 4
- Pairs: 8
- Time: 60 seconds

## PRO

- Grid: 4 × 5
- Pairs: 10
- Time: 75 seconds

## ELITE

- Grid: 6 × 6
- Pairs: 18
- Time: 120 seconds

## Turn rules

A player selects two hidden cards.

If the pair IDs match:

- both cards remain revealed
- moves increase
- matches increase
- combo increases
- score increases

If they do not match:

- moves increase
- mistakes increase
- combo resets
- score receives a penalty
- cards return face-down after a short delay

## Win

A player wins when all pairs have been matched before time reaches zero.

## Loss

A player loses when the timer reaches zero before all pairs are matched.

## Pause

Pause stops the timer and prevents card selection.

## Restart

Restart creates a new shuffled deck and resets the mission.
